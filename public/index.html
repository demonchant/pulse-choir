THE PULSE CHOIR DIFFERENCE

Not a song request.
A song made by chat.

No imported tracks, clips or samples. Each contribution is a short, original tone, mapped by a deterministic rule and played on the shared beat.

ACCESS IN TWO WAYS

See the rhythm.
Read the music.

For Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers: the broadcast visualizer shows a four-beat position, accepted note names, tempo, key and scale as text. Sound is never the only signal.

For blind and low-vision viewers: share the separate companion page for labeled musical state, a readable note list, keyboard controls and paced screen-reader announcements. The OBS graphic in the Twitch video is not screen-reader accessible to viewers.

For nonspeaking viewers: participation uses ordinary text chat commands; voice input is not required. Pulse Choir supports these parts of the experience and does not claim to make a stream generally accessible.
